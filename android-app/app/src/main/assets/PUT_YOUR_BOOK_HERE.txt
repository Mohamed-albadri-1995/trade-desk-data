Put your PDF book in THIS folder and name it exactly:

    book.pdf

That's it. When you Run the app in Android Studio, it will open this file.

Notes:
- The name must be lowercase "book.pdf". If you want a different name,
  change the `assetFileName` line in
  app/src/main/java/com/pdfbook/reader/MainActivity.kt
- You can delete this .txt file once your book.pdf is in place.
